Setup and troubleshooting
  1. Open the Research Library Mac app and select your library folder.
  2. Run Install Chrome Helper.command from this extension’s folder.
  3. Pin Save to Research Library using Chrome’s Extensions menu.

↓ means the article metadata has been handed to the app and the PDF is downloading. ✓ means the PDF has reached Inbox and the app has been notified to attach it immediately. Text indexing continues in the background.

PDF? means the metadata was retained but the PDF download needs attention. The app shows the download issue. Publisher access restrictions still apply. Streaming supports PDFs up to 512 MB; download time depends on the publisher and connection.

This extension uses a local Mac helper. It has no cloud service and reads a page only when you click its icon. It uses your browser session when fetching that PDF; it does not extract or send cookies to the helper.
